Question: What is the total storage capacity of the TSF reported in the technical report?

Answer:
Total storage capacity is the sum of the storage capacities of the tailings storage facility and tailings reclaim sump. The storage capacity for tailings reclaimed sump is not included in the total capacity because it is not a tailings facility. The total capacity of tailings reclamation sump can be calculated as follows:

Total storage capacity = (Storage capacity of TSF + Storage capacity of TWSP)
