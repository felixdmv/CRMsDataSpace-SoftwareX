Question: What materials are present in the waste facility?

Answer:
The waste facility is composed of gold, silver, copper, lead, zinc, tin, tungsten, molybdenum, antimony, manganese, beryllium, boron, bismuth, arsenic, cadmium, chromium, cobalt, iron, nickel, palladium, platinum, rhodium, ruthenium, selenium, thallium, titanium, uranium, vanadium, and zirconium. These metals are found in the tailings. The tailings are also composed of other metals, such as aluminum, magnesium, sodium, potassium, calcium, sulfur, and sodium sulfate. The metals are also found in some of the waste materials that are stored in the facility. Some of these metals are used in the production of other materials.
