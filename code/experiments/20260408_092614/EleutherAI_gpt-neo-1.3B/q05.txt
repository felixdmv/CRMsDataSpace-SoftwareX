Question: According to 2023LUGTechnicalReport.pdf, what is the operating philosophy for the TSF?

Answer:
The TSF's operating philosophy is based on the following principles:
1) Tailings and sludge are stored in the tailings pond.
2) Decant ponds are used to store sludge and tailings.
3) The tailings are treated with a cyanide destruction process.
4) The sludge is treated with an electrostatic precipitator.
5) The reclaimed barge is used to transport tailings, sludge, and decant ponds.
6) The operating conditions are designed to achieve the following objectives:
a) Reduce the amount of tailings discharged into the environment.
b) Increase the volume of reclaimed water.
c) Reduce energy consumption.
d) Reduce capital costs.
e) Reduce operating and maintenance expenses.
f) Reduce environmental impacts.
g) Reduce operational and maintenance risks.
h) Reduce maintenance and repair costs.
