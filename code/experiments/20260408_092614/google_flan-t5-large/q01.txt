Question: What materials are present in the waste facility?

Answer:
Waste Management takes place in all the infrastructures of the mine operation. A waste management center was built to temporarily store the generated classified waste. Hazardous waste is managed through a Waste Management center. The mine is located in a remote part of the Amazon rainforest. It is surrounded by a dense forest.
